# NUTRITION FIRE — Modern DTC Sports Nutrition Storefront

Welcome to the official web platform for **NUTRITION FIRE**, a premium fitness and supplement e-commerce brand engineered with a modern Gen-Z athletic aesthetic (inspired by Gymshark, Ghost Lifestyle, and Nike DTC).

---

## 🚀 Quick Launch

Double click **`index.html`** in your file explorer to launch the storefront in any web browser.

---

## 🎨 Modern DTC Visual Design System

1. **Sophisticated Neutral Palette with Electric Blue Accent**:
   - Deep Black (`#08090B`) & Near Black (`#0F1115`)
   - Surface Dark Cards (`#111318`) & Subtle Card Surface (`#181A20`)
   - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) & Off-White Background (`#F8F8F8`)
   - Cool Grays (`#A1A1AA`, `#71717A`)
   - Clean Micro-Borders (`#1F2229` in Dark Mode, `#E4E4E7` in Light Mode)
   - Electric Blue Accent (`#3B82F6`) used sparingly for focused hierarchy
   - **Zero orange text, zero fire gradients, and zero gaming clutter**.

2. **Refined Typography**:
   - **Space Grotesk**: High-contrast geometric headings with tight letter spacing (`-0.03em`).
   - **Inter**: Clean, readable, modern body copy and specs.

3. **Editorial Category Showcase**:
   - 5 dedicated editorial cards highlighting Whey Protein, Creatine, Pre-Workout, Mass Gainer, and Global Brands with authentic product imagery and seamless 1-click filter activation.

4. **Atmospheric Background Texture**:
   - The aggressive screen-flashing lightning has been redesigned into an ultra-subtle, elegant electric texture with low opacity that never distracts from typography or products.

5. **Minimal Modern Custom Cursor**:
   - Sleek 5px precision center dot with a smooth 26px ring that smoothly expands upon hovering over interactive elements.

## 💬 WhatsApp Direct Checkout

- Customer builds their cart and proceeds to checkout.
- Automatically compiles a clean, structured order receipt:
  - Customer Name, Phone, and Full Shipping Address
  - Itemized products, flavors, sizes, quantities, and prices
  - Promo code discounts (`FIRE10`), shipping status, and final total
- Redirects directly to `https://wa.me/<your_number>` in one click!
- **To update your WhatsApp number**: Open **Studio (`⚙️`)** in the navbar or footer, click **WhatsApp Settings**, and enter your number with country code (e.g. `919876543210`).

## ✏️ 1-Click Price & Photo Quick Edit

- Click the **✏️** icon on any product card on the homepage to instantly edit:
  - Product Title
  - Selling Price (₹) & Original MRP (₹)
  - Product Photo (upload directly from your PC with instant preview)
- Click **Studio (`⚙️`)** to add new products across all 5 divisions or export catalog JSON backups.
